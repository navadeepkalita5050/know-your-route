        How it works

        • Roads: National Highways plus Assam, West Bengal and Mizoram state roads from OpenStreetMap, joined into one network. A few short data gaps are bridged on an indicative line, flagged on the route sheet.
        • Distance: measured along that network, with a small allowance for bends on hill roads.
        • Time: estimated from lane type and terrain (how winding the road is), for a car or a loaded truck, without traffic or stops.
        • Lanes and agency: MoRTH National Highway records on PM GatiShakti, June 2022.
        • Post offices: India Post directory. — PIN codes have a usable location and can be routed to. The rest are searchable, but only their district is known.
        • Local roads: from a place to the nearest highway, estimated as straight-line distance × 1.3.
